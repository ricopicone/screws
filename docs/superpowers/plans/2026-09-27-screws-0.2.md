# `screws` 0.2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship `screws` 0.2: MR chapter 8 (dynamics) and 9 (trajectories) with aliases and `Robot` methods, `Arm.robot(inertias=True)` reading masses and inertias off the scene, and a `Recorder` that saves a one-frame-per-step movie of a run.

**Architecture:** `dynamics.py` and `trajectory.py` are pure numpy ports of MR under the spec's names; `Robot`'s dynamics methods stop raising and delegate. The bridge grows `Arm.link_inertias()` (grouping shapes by nearest joint ancestor, parallel-axis combination) and a `video.py` with `Recorder`, both tested against the fake sim, then live.

**Tech Stack:** as 0.1, plus the `video` extra: `imageio>=2.31`, `imageio-ffmpeg>=0.4`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-screws-package-design.md` §3 (name table), §4 (Robot), §8 (scene inertias, Recorder), §9 (the inertia lesson).

## Global Constraints

As in the 0.1 plan, plus: MR's dynamics defaults are kept (`forward_dynamics_trajectory` uses Euler with `int_res` substeps); trajectories are N x n arrays with `N` rows, MR's row layout; `scaling` is `"cubic"` or `"quintic"` and the MR aliases accept `3`/`5`.

## Review Focus

1. `inverse_dynamics` with `F_tip=None` must mean a zero wrench, and the `Robot` methods must use `robot.gravity`. (Task 1)
2. `joint_trajectory` with `N=1` must not divide by zero (return the start row). (Task 2)
3. Shapes that are static (visual shells) must be excluded from scene inertias; a link with no dynamic shape must raise, not produce a zero-mass link silently. (Task 3)
4. Frames from `getVisionSensorImg` are bottom-up; the recorder must flip them so movies are upright. (Task 4)
5. `Recorder.save` on a path with an unknown extension must raise before the run, not after. (Task 4)
